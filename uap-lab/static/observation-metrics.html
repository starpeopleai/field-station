Composite score

A custom score from 0 to 100. Higher is better.

  • Recall: 35% — finds anomalies
  • Precision: 25% — alerts that are anomalous
  • Specificity: 25% — rejects normal objects
  • Accuracy: 15% — correct classifications overall

Uses a weighted harmonic mean, so weak results pull the score down:

100 ÷ (0.35 ÷ recall + 0.25 ÷ precision + 0.25 ÷ specificity + 0.15 ÷ accuracy)

Rates use 0–1 values. Specificity = 1 − false-positive rate. Any zero component gives a score of 0. Results missing either class are unranked.

Uses the shared evaluation set; reviewed-live results are separate. Scores round to one decimal, and ties share a rank. This is not a probability or unseen-data accuracy.
